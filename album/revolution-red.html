Notes

  • The album was illustrated by Ichika.
  • mothy noted on Twitter that the songs in the album aren't necessarily in chronological order.
  • The album booklet features the names of the focus characters and a brief description. The name of the character for Revolution Red is censored.
  • The digital release of the album only features the four new songs (Revolution Red, The Imperial Princess Who Slumbers in the Grave, the Wicked Woman Who Guards It, Evil Food Eater Conchita (2026 MEIKO Only Ver), and Conflict – Enbizaka) along with their Off Vocal versions.
